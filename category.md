# Sunflower Petals: Product Categories

Our collection is thoughtfully curated into three core ranges, each designed to support neurodiverse children through the magic of purposeful play and structured living.

---

## 1. Sensory Regulation Tools
**Focus: Calm, Focus, and Tactile Input**

These tools are designed to provide consistent, predictable sensory feedback. They help manage restless energy and provide a grounding tactile experience for children who benefit from sensory seeking or regulation.

*   **Key Benefits**: Self-regulation, focus & attention, fine motor development.
*   **Featured Products**: 
    *   **Clicky-Clacky**: A satisfying tactile tool using mechanical switches.
    *   **Sensory Spinner**: A controlled-motion tool for deliberate movement and focus.

---

## 2. Organization and Routine Aids
**Focus: Predictability, Flow, and Visual Structure**

Stability comes from understanding what comes next. Our routine aids turn abstract concepts like "time" and "schedule" into hands-on, interactive experiences that reduce transition anxiety and build confidence.

*   **Key Benefits**: Routine building, sequencing skills, reduced anxiety during transitions.
*   **Featured Products**:
    *   **Sunflower Calendar**: A customizable, tactile calendar for learning dates and daily flows.

---

## 3. Skill-Building and Independence
**Focus: Cognitive Growth, Life Skills, and Independent Learning**

We believe that learning should be as tactile as it is mental. This range focuses on foundational skills—from early mathematics to daily living tasks—presented in an engaging, child-led format.

*   **Key Benefits**: Early math skills, problem-solving, cognitive development, functional independence.
*   **Featured Products**:
    *   **Tactile Number Blocks**: Stackable, colorful blocks for visual and hands-on mathematical operations.

---

> [!TIP]
> All Sunflower Petals products are 3D-printed using professional-grade, bio-degradable PLA, ensuring they are as kind to the planet as they are to your child's sensory needs.
